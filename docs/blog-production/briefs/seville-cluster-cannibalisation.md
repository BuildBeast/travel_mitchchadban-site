# Seville Cluster — Cannibalisation Plan

_Prepared 2026-07-29 · Covers the two existing Seville pages and the five new ones · Companion: [`seville-3-days-brief.md`](./seville-3-days-brief.md)_

The cluster has seven pages about one city. They stay distinct because each answers a **different reader question**, not because each covers different sights. Overlap of *subject* is unavoidable and fine; overlap of *job* is the failure mode.

## Rules the cluster is held to

1. **One primary intent per page.** No page targets another's head term in its title, H1 or meta description.
2. **The two incumbents are not rewritten.** `/3-days-in-seville/` and `/10-days-in-sevilla/` receive reciprocal links only.
3. **Routes live in two places only** — `/3-days-in-seville/` and `/one-day-in-seville/`. No other page in the cluster emits an ordered day plan.
4. **Restaurant names live in two places only** — `/10-days-in-sevilla/` (the personal record) and `/seville-tapas-food-guide/` (the system, with evidence classes). No route page carries a restaurant list.
5. **Neighbourhood assessment lives in one place** — `/best-areas-to-stay-in-seville/`. Other pages give at most one sentence plus a link.
6. **Day-trip verdicts live in one place** — `/best-day-trips-from-seville/`. Other pages say "not on this trip length" plus a link.
7. **No page claims Mitch performed a route.** Firsthand material is attributed to the documented 2025 stay and linked to the chronicle that records it.

## Where the boundary is thinnest, and the test applied

The two genuine risks are **one-day vs three-day** and **things-to-do vs three-day**. The test used on both:

> Could a paragraph be moved between the two pages without the reader noticing it had moved?

If yes, it is written in the wrong place. Applied concretely:

- The three-day page can say *"give the Alcázar two hours"*. The one-day page must say *"give the Alcázar two hours, and accept that this is why you will not see Triana"* — the cost is the content.
- The three-day page can say *"then walk north to Casa de Pilatos"*. The things-to-do page must say *"Casa de Pilatos or Lebrija or Dueñas — here is how to choose one, and why one is enough on a first visit"* — the choice is the content.
