# Article QA Checklist

## Provenance
- [ ] Every first-person or traveller-experience statement is supported by supplied material.
- [ ] No hotel stay, meal, journey, price, encounter or emotion has been invented.
- [ ] Current operational facts have primary-source support.
- [ ] Unsupported claims are removed or visibly flagged.

## Search and architecture
- [ ] The page has one clear primary intent.
- [ ] It does not duplicate the destination cornerstone.
- [ ] Title, H1, slug and metadata are distinct but aligned.
- [ ] Internal links point to the cornerstone and relevant siblings.
- [ ] Existing paired or route posts are linked where appropriate.

## Usefulness
- [ ] The answer begins early.
- [ ] Routes and day plans are geographically realistic.
- [ ] Travel and dwell times are plausible.
- [ ] Trade-offs are explicit.
- [ ] Practical details are scannable.
- [ ] No section exists merely to increase length.

## Voice
- [ ] The prose is measured and specific.
- [ ] The page avoids influencer commands and hype.
- [ ] No purple prose or fake profundity.
- [ ] No generic AI-travel phrases.
- [ ] The place could not be swapped for another city without substantial rewriting.

## Food
- [ ] Food is culturally grounded.
- [ ] Any venue in prose appears in the practical layer.
- [ ] Service times and reservation advice are verified or qualified.

## Technical integration
- [ ] Frontmatter matches the repository schema.
- [ ] Components and shortcodes follow existing conventions.
- [ ] No shared styles, routes or schemas changed without approval.
- [ ] Links resolve.
- [ ] Images exist and alt text matches the actual image.
- [ ] Build and relevant checks pass.

## Final report
- [ ] Files created/changed listed.
- [ ] Sources listed.
- [ ] Assumptions listed.
- [ ] Remaining Mitch decisions listed.
- [ ] No commit, push or deployment performed unless requested.
