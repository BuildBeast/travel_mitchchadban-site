# Pilot Brief — 3 Days in Seville

## Goal

Create the missing standalone Seville cornerstone that supports the existing 10-day Andalucía itinerary and becomes the hub for future Seville accommodation, one-day, attractions, day-trip and food guides.

## Current content relationship

Existing page:

- `10 Days in Sevilla: Andalucía Without Rush`

The new page must be a city-only three-day itinerary. It must not simply extract and rephrase the Seville sections of the regional article.

## Primary reader decision

“How should I spend three days in Seville without rushing the city or arranging the days badly?”

## Required research

- Existing Seville and Andalucía content in the repository.
- Any user photographs, notes, tickets, accommodation records, restaurant notes or route evidence related to Seville.
- Official attraction sources for current booking and closure rules.
- Official local and regional transport sources.
- Geographic validation of each day’s route.

## Proposed route hypothesis

This is a research hypothesis, not an instruction to state unsupported facts:

- Day 1: monumental core and Santa Cruz.
- Day 2: Triana, river and central food/market layer.
- Day 3: northern/alternative Seville, parks or a slower thematic day depending on the user’s actual source material.

Claude must validate or replace this structure after inspecting the sources.

## Supporting-page boundaries

The cornerstone may summarise, but must not exhaust:

- neighbourhood-by-neighbourhood accommodation advice;
- every attraction in the city;
- all possible Seville day trips;
- a full tapas taxonomy or restaurant directory;
- a detailed one-day sprint.

Those belong to later cluster pages.

## Pilot stop point

For the first run, produce only:

1. Repository/content-system audit.
2. Research packet.
3. Proposed article outline.
4. SEO package.
5. Internal-link plan.
6. List of missing Mitch inputs.

Do not draft or modify the live article until Mitch approves the packet.
