# Research Note — Seville Tapas and Food Guide

_Prepared 2026-07-29 · All external sources accessed 2026-07-29 · Route: `/seville-tapas-food-guide/`_

**Provenance key:** **[REPO]** repository content · **[PHOTO]** photographs + EXIF · **[PRIMARY]** official page read · **[SECONDARY]** reputable specialist source · **[INFERENCE]** labelled reasoning

## Purpose

Answer: **how should a visitor understand and eat through Seville without reducing its food culture to a restaurant list?** Food as cultural structure, neighbourhood rhythm, ritual and practical knowledge — the project's stated food philosophy. The restaurant section is deliberately the smallest part of the page.

## Key decisions

1. **Four evidence classes, kept visibly separate.** This is the article's single most important structural rule:
   - **A — Places Mitch ate.** Named, attributed to the documented Sept–Oct 2025 stay, linked to `/10-days-in-sevilla/`.
   - **B — Dishes personally documented.** Exactly one venue's dishes are recorded: the slow-cooked egg, the oxtail croquette and the glass of fino at Eslava **[REPO]**. Nothing else.
   - **C — Current researched recommendation.** Institutions and categories that can be verified as operating, presented as research, never as experience.
   - **D — Wider cultural explanation.** How the system works. No provenance problem; also the bulk of the article.
2. **The large restaurant table from `/10-days-in-sevilla/` is not copied.** Thirteen venues plus a bar are recorded there. This page names only those that carry a *specific point about how Seville eats*, and links back for the full record.
3. **Lead with the mechanics, not the list.** The opening third explains the ordering system, the vocabulary of quantity (tapa / media ración / ración), the counter-versus-table price difference, and the clock. That is the knowledge a visitor actually lacks.
4. **The clock is treated as the central fact.** Lunch 14:00–15:30, dinner rarely before 21:00, and the near-total dead zone between about 16:30 and 20:00 in traditional bars. More visitor meals are ruined by the clock than by ordering badly.
5. **"Tapas are not free in Seville"** is stated early and plainly, because the Granada convention **[REPO]** is the single most common false expectation, and this site's own Granada material is where a reader may have picked it up.
6. **No FAQ section**, therefore no FAQPage schema. No affiliate links.

## Cultural facts used, and their standing

| Claim | Standing |
|---|---|
| Tapas in Seville are ordered and paid for; they are not given free with a drink | Stable cultural fact; the contrast case (Granada) is documented in this site's own material **[REPO]** |
| Quantity vocabulary: *tapa* → *media ración* → *ración* | Stable |
| Standing at the bar is generally cheaper than a table, and a terrace table cheaper than nothing at all only in comfort | Stable convention; stated as a convention, not a tariff |
| The Sevillian clock: lunch ~14:00–15:30, dinner from ~21:00, a dead zone in traditional bars roughly 16:30–20:00 | Stable; corroborated by the 2025 photographic record, which shows an evening restaurant frame at 14:15 and dinner-hour frames after 19:00 **[PHOTO]** |
| Core Sevillian dishes: *espinacas con garbanzos*, *carrillada*, *solomillo al whisky*, *cola de toro*, *pescaíto frito*, *salmorejo*, *montadito*, *serranito*, *tortilla de camarones* on the coast | Stable regional repertoire. Described by what they are, not attributed as eaten |
| *Salmorejo* is thicker and made without the pepper and cucumber of *gazpacho* | Stable |
| Sherry from the Marco de Jerez: *fino*, *manzanilla*, *amontillado*, *oloroso*, *Pedro Ximénez*, and the solera system | Stable; *fino* is the one documented as drunk **[REPO]** |
| Triana's market stands on the foundations of the castle of San Jorge | Established in `/3-days-in-seville/` **[REPO]** |
| Convent sweets sold through a revolving hatch | Documented firsthand at Yemas de San Leandro **[REPO]** |
| *Abacería* = a deli-tavern that sells and serves the same goods | Stable; documented at Abacería del Postigo **[REPO]** |

## Refresh-sensitive facts and phrasing

| Fact | Phrasing strategy |
|---|---|
| Mercado de Triana hours | Given as the **retail-versus-hospitality distinction**, which is the durable and decision-relevant shape, with times behind a verified-on date |
| Restaurant opening hours and closing days | **Not quoted for any venue.** The article gives the city-wide clock and tells readers to check the individual place, because Sevillian bars close for holidays and August with little notice |
| Whether a named venue is still trading | Every named venue is presented as *recorded in 2025* or *long-established*, never as verified-open today. The article states this rule visibly |
| Prices | No restaurant price is quoted. The only euro figures on the page are the ones legible in the photographs, described as what the price cards read in October 2025 |
| Michelin notes in the ten-day chronicle | Reported as a 2025 record, not current status |
| Reservation practice | Given as a rule of thumb (book the sit-down kitchens, never the counters) rather than per-venue policy |
