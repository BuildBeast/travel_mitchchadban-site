Iguazú was not one park visited twice. It was two long days arranged around the same immense landscape, with a border crossing between them.

The base was Puerto Iguazú, in Argentina. The first full day stayed on the Argentine side, moving through Garganta Station, Estación Cataratas, the Upper Circuit and the Lower Circuit before ending at a hummingbird garden. The second crossed into Brazil for the Cataratas trail, a recorded Macuco Safari stop and Parque das Aves.

That division gives the trip its shape. Argentina was the more explicitly segmented park day in the itinerary. Brazil combined a falls trail with a separate wildlife visit. Neither day needs to be reduced to a winner. With two days, the useful decision is to do both.

## Iguazú in Two Days at a Glance

- **26 September:** arrive from Mendoza, settle in at La Rioja 718, visit Hito Tres Fronteras and eat at El Hito Restaurante Mirador.
- **27 September:** Argentina — Iguazú National Park, Garganta Station, Estación Cataratas, Upper Circuit, Lower Circuit, Central Station / Jungle Train exit, Jardín de los Picaflores and Antigal Parrilla.
- **28 September:** Brazil — border/police stop, Visitor Center Station, Trilha das Cataratas Brasil, Macuco Safari and Parque das Aves, then return to Puerto Iguazú.
- **29 September:** departure to Buenos Aires.

The two days are complementary because the itinerary itself gives them different jobs.

**Argentina:** the route is more explicitly segmented. Garganta Station, Estación Cataratas, the Upper Circuit, the Lower Circuit and Central Station create a chain of park stops, followed by Jardín de los Picaflores.

**Brazil:** the route combines the Cataratas trail with a recorded Macuco Safari stop and Parque das Aves. Its day also carries the extra logistics of outward and return border-related stops.

**Wildlife:** Jardín de los Picaflores is recorded on the Argentina day. Parque das Aves is recorded and visually confirmed on the Brazil day.

**Boat component:** no boat activity is recorded in the Argentina route. Macuco Safari is recorded on the Brazil route, although the surviving material does not establish what the experience included.

The source material does not establish a better side. It does establish that, on this itinerary, the two countries supplied distinct day structures rather than interchangeable entrances to the same route.

## Is Two Days Enough?

For this recorded route, two days were enough to visit both sides and include the principal named stops in the itinerary. They were not two interchangeable sightseeing loops.
