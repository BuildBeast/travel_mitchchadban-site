## Which Santiago viewpoint is best?

There is no evidence for a single objective winner, and the recorded visit does not preserve a direct ranking. The more useful answer is to choose by the kind of city view you want:

- **Choose San Cristóbal Hill** if the point is to place Santiago in its larger hill and mountain setting.
- **Choose Santa Lucía Hill** if you want the viewpoint to remain tied to the central city and its park-like spaces.
- **Choose Sky Costanera** if you want the most continuous built panorama represented by the three recorded subjects.

Seeing all three is defensible when the purpose is comparison rather than efficiency. They are not three versions of the same stop: the route moves from hill and landscape, to central urban elevation, to a high built view. The precise sequence remains unresolved, so I would not present one order as the route that definitely happened.
